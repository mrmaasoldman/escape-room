The Astronomer's Study

Professor Halvorsen locked the door behind you. You can't take a single step. All you can do is turn around and look.

  1. Hold your phone up like a window and turn your body to look around. Look up at the ceiling and down at the floor too.
  2. Pinch to zoom in on anything far away.
  3. Tap an object to inspect it.
  4. Work out the five-digit code that opens the door.

Works best standing up, where you can turn all the way around.

Door unlocked

You're out.

The lock clicks, the door swings open and cool night air pours into the study.
