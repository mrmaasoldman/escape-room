Escaped

Every room hides a way out somewhere in its puzzles. You can't take a single step. Look around, solve what you find and escape.

  1. Look around the whole room, including the ceiling and the floor. Turn your phone to look, or drag the screen with touch controls.
  2. Pinch to zoom in on anything far away.
  3. Tap an object to inspect it.
  4. Work out the five-digit code that opens the door.

Phone motion works best standing up, where you can turn all the way around. Touch controls work sitting down or on a computer. You can switch any time with the button at the top of the screen.
